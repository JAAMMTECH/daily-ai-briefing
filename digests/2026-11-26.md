# AI + Ontario briefing — November 26, 2026

llama.cpp shipped a major release, and several new papers report measured gains in robot control, image generation, and medical multimodal models. In Canada, the Senate reworked a lawful-access bill, 1Password described a backend intrusion that left customer vaults intact, and Northern Ontario's innovation centres formed a joint AI and defence effort.

## AI

### ggml-org/llama.cpp v1.1.0

GitHub: ggml-org/llama.cpp

llama.cpp v1.1.0, tagged today, adds the Inkling 2 family (base, Mini, and DSpark), Domyn Edge 4B, Zaya1 8B, RAON-1-mini, Hy4 flash, and a set of speech and music models, and it splits DFlash speculative decoding into separate draft methods. It deprecates the old llama_batch API in favour of llama_batch_ext, adds automatic per-tensor quantization aimed at a bits-per-weight or size budget, and includes a slot-scheduling refactor plus a redesigned Llama Chat web UI with voice messages and agent traces.

Why it's worth knowing: This is the runtime many people use to run open models on their own hardware, so new model support and a deprecated batch API show up in everyday local inference. The release notes describe those changes and do not include a speed figure.

Terms:
- **API** — application programming interface — The functions other programs call to run llama.cpp. This release deprecates the older batch call.

https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/v1.1.0

### PyRUA-Dex: Learning Programmatic Policies for Dexterous Bimanual Manipulation with Coding Agents

Hugging Face papers

PyRUA-Dex has coding agents write dexterous-manipulation programs without demonstrations: several agents explore in parallel, a distilling agent turns the successes into one program, and that program is then refined on randomized layouts. It scores 72.9 on Bench2Dex and reaches 81.0 percent success on DexJoCo under full randomization, ahead of every trained policy on both benchmarks, and on a real bimanual platform with two 22-degree-of-freedom hands it learned a new piano piece from sheet music in as little as 30 minutes.

Why it's worth knowing: Dexterous robot skills are usually limited by how hard it is to collect good demonstrations. A lead over trained policies, plus a timed result on real hands, gives a concrete sense of what program search can do instead.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.33144

### DyLoMo-DiT: Dynamic Looped Mixture-of-Experts Diffusion Transformers

Hugging Face papers

DyLoMo-DiT deepens a diffusion transformer by looping shared middle layers and routing tokens through sparse experts, and it spends more passes on low-noise steps. On ImageNet at 256 by 256, at matched parameter and compute budgets, it reduces FID by 11 to 24 percent versus a plain diffusion transformer and by 36 to 56 percent versus a mixture-of-experts diffusion transformer; a scaled text-to-image version scores 0.714 on GenEval against FLUX.1 at 0.673, with less than one tenth of FLUX.1's active parameters.

Why it's worth knowing: The useful claim is the matched budget: reuse and extra low-noise passes beat simply adding parameters on these tests. The FLUX comparison gives a familiar text-to-image reference point for the same idea at larger scale.

Terms:
- **FID** — Fréchet Inception Distance — A score of how far generated images sit from real ones. Lower is closer to the real set.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.30844

### HOG-WAM: Improving Visual Robustness through Future Gradient Prediction

Hugging Face papers

HOG-WAM adds future histograms of oriented gradients as training supervision for a world-action model, through a module that is removed at inference so the controller still maps camera images to actions. Mixed-noise success on a RoboTwin corruption suite rises from 78.86 percent to 82.86 percent versus FastWAM, sensor-noise success on LIBERO Plus rises from 74.14 percent to 78.89 percent, and across four Franka tasks average success goes from 40 percent to 59 percent in clean scenes and from 17 percent to 24 percent in noisy ones.

Why it's worth knowing: Overall LIBERO Plus success moves only from 74.49 percent to 75.26 percent, so the clearer signal is the noise cases and the Franka tasks. The extra module does not have to run on the robot.

Terms:
- **HOG** — histogram of oriented gradients — A description of edges and their directions in an image. The paper uses future HOG maps as a structural training target, then drops that head at runtime.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34811

### Sage-Med: Scaling Medical Multimodal Intelligence

Hugging Face papers

Sage-Med-Preview is a 35-billion-parameter medical multimodal model with 3 billion parameters active, built from a curriculum over 322.7 million candidate records. The authors compare it with 77 models on 238 clinical tasks from 61 public and 13 private multi-centre benchmarks in more than 40 modalities, and they report consistent stage-wise gains from supervised refinement and adaptive reinforcement learning.

Why it's worth knowing: The width of the evaluation is what a reader can use now: a small active model, a very large candidate pool, and a mix of public and private clinical tests. The visible abstract reports those stage-wise gains and does not include the final margins against the 77 models.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34575

### Mixture-of-Loops Transformer

Hugging Face papers

Mixture-of-Loops Transformer reuses layers by passing keys and values across loops, and a U-shaped variant builds that memory separately from the prediction readout so it can skip decoder passes it will not use. Pretrained on 400 billion tokens, that U-shaped model outperformed OLMo3 at 700 million and 1.5 billion dense parameters and at a 3-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts size.

Why it's worth knowing: Looping spends more compute on a smaller set of weights. The abstract states the win over OLMo3 at those three sizes and cuts off before the size of the gap.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34923

### Playfulness as Feedback for Agentic Game Development

Hugging Face papers

Play2Evolve has coding agents playtest the games they generate and use notes on function, visuals, and gameplay to fix bugs, improve assets, and set the next goals, mixing a video model with code for static and moving art. It scores 60.05 on GameCraft-Bench, compared with 50.81 after initialization alone and 52.46 for Play2Code.

Why it's worth knowing: A game that runs is a weak test of whether anyone would want to play it. The bench gap is evidence that playtesting feedback moved the score against these two baselines.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.33457

### DocSEAM: Bidirectional Cross-Page Encoding for Multi-Page Document Understanding

Hugging Face papers

DocSEAM sits between a vision encoder and a language model and, for each page, mixes in the two neighboring pages, then returns only the enriched centre page so the visual token count stays the same. After a cross-page warm-up, with DocSEAM frozen during multi-page fine-tuning, a 9-billion-parameter model leads the open-weight models of up to 10 billion parameters that the authors evaluated on five multi-page document question-answering benchmarks.

Why it's worth knowing: Encoding each page alone drops sentences and tables that cross a page break. This is a small add-on with a stated comparison set, and the abstract does not include the point margins.

Terms:
- **DocSEAM** — Document Selective Enrichment via Anchored Masking — A module that lets one page's visual tokens take context from the previous and next pages, then keeps only the centre page's tokens.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34420

## Ontario and Canada

### Senate committee recommends changing Liberals' data access bill following cybersecurity concerns

CBC News

The Senate national security committee amended Bill C-22, which would require technology companies to preserve data for police and CSIS when those agencies have a warrant and to retain user metadata for up to six months. Tuesday's amendment treats a credible risk that secure information could be accessed as a systemic vulnerability, and Wednesday's lets a provider ask the public safety minister to consult a new technical advisory committee, while the six-month metadata rule stayed in place.

Why it's worth knowing: Windscribe, a Toronto VPN that promises not to store user data, told the committee it will move out of Canada if the bill passes. Senators still have to vote, and any amended bill goes back to the House, where the Liberals hold a majority.

Terms:
- **CSIS** — Canadian Security Intelligence Service — Canada's civilian intelligence service. Under the bill it could receive preserved data when it has a warrant.
- **VPN** — virtual private network — A service that carries a user's traffic through an encrypted tunnel. Windscribe's objection is that the bill would force it to store data it currently says it does not keep.

https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/bill-c-22-lawful-access-security-9.7436101

### Update on a security incident involving 1Password’s backend systems

1Password

1Password says that on November 19 an attacker used an employee's corporate GitHub account to push malicious code into backend services and copy account records for a subset of customers, including names, sign-in emails, identifiers, public keys, and some invitation and recovery tokens. The company says vault contents and the keys that protect them stayed inaccessible, that it does not store account passwords or secret keys, and that those tokens still require single sign-on or an email code, now ten alphanumeric characters rather than six digits.

Why it's worth knowing: 1Password is a Toronto company, so the stolen names and emails are a Canadian phishing risk even with the vaults left intact. Customers were told to leave their account settings unchanged and to be wary of unexpected invitation or recovery email.

https://1password.com/blog/update-on-nov-2026-security-incident
