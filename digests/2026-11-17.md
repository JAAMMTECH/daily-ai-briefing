## AI

### Windows 11 is collecting data on which coding agents and developer tools you use

Hacker News

Windows 11's MissionControlAggregator.dll records which coding agents and developer tools have run, including Claude, Codex, Copilot, Cursor, and Antigravity, and reports that through Windows Diagnostics. Optional diagnostic data is enabled by default, so the report is uploaded unless that setting is turned off.

Why it's worth knowing: Anyone using a coding agent on Windows can be telling Microsoft which tools they run, because the optional diagnostics switch that sends the report is on unless someone changed it.

### K2-Horizon: A Frontier Fleet of Open Source Language Models

Hugging Face papers

K2-Horizon is a fleet of six fully open language models, from a 0.9-billion-parameter dense model to a 375-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts with 23 billion active parameters. Its 36B-A4B model, using Mixture-of-Value Attention and softplus attention gating, nearly matches a 32-billion dense model trained on the same data with one-seventh the active parameters, and Uno adapters speed decoding by up to 2.2 times without a draft model.

Why it's worth knowing: The release includes intermediate checkpoints, pre-training data, logs, and training code, so the attention change and the speedup can be checked instead of taken from a leaderboard line.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.21003

### Sage S2.1 Flash Technical Report

Hugging Face papers

The Sage S2.1 Flash technical report introduces Sage S1.5 Flash, a 320-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with encoders for molecular graphs, biological sequences, and spectra. In a comparison against five baselines it ranked first on OfficeQA Pro (72.2), MLE-bench Lite (90.3 HumanRank), Harvey LAB, and SpreadsheetBench 2, and second on OSWorld-Verified (85.2), WebArena-Verified (67.6), and DeepResearch Bench II (54.2).

Why it's worth knowing: The strong scores are on office work, spreadsheets, and machine-learning experiments that can be checked against an environment, not only on chat benchmarks.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.21072

### ScaleVLA: Scaling Vision-Language-Action Models to 552 Billion Parameters

Hugging Face papers

ScaleVLA connects a 552-billion-parameter DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash backbone to a trainable action diffusion transformer and compares backbones from 2 billion to 552 billion parameters under one training setup. Ten demonstrations beat a 2-billion-parameter model trained on 50 (87.8 percent versus 85.6 percent), LIBERO-PRO success reached 45.1 percent against at most 31.3 percent for smaller backbones, and real charger insertion improved from 4 of 10 to 9 of 10, at a median 216 milliseconds per action chunk.

Why it's worth knowing: The paper's claim is that a frontier backbone can save robot demonstrations, and the charger result is a contact task rather than another simulated score.

Terms:
- **VLA** — vision-language-action — A robot policy that maps camera images and language to physical actions.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.21607

### InclusionMed: Benchmarking AI for Inclusive Healthcare

Hugging Face papers

InclusionMed's first index scores frontier models from 17.5 to 49.1 out of 100 across inclusive-healthcare benchmarks, and rank order changes with the clinical role or the capability being measured. The ten highest-ranked models all scored higher on patient communication than on clinical decision-making.

Why it's worth knowing: A single medical score hides a split: the same models look better when they talk with patients than when they have to decide.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.21218

### We built a chip prototype that runs Qwen 27B. You can try it live.

r/LocalLLM

Valley Semiconductor says an FPGA prototype in AWS is running the full Qwen 27B model at 4-bit weights, about 7.9 tokens a second on short prompts and 5.5 in live chat, with every output matching its 4-bit reference and quality within 2.5 percent of full precision. Those figures are from the prototype; the custom chip is described as not yet demonstrated.

Why it's worth knowing: The live session is a vendor demo of 27-billion-parameter inference on reconfigurable hardware, not a shipped accelerator, and the exact match to a 4-bit reference is the checkable part.

Terms:

### AllocFlow: End-to-End Portfolio Trading Action Generation from Raw Market Data

Hugging Face papers

AllocFlow generates signed weights for a whole portfolio at once from raw multi-asset market history, using a cross-asset flow Transformer rather than a separate forecast-then-allocate pipeline. It first imitates example strategies, then refines the joint positions with group-relative reinforcement learning against portfolio-level rewards.

Why it's worth knowing: The training target is the allocation itself, under a shared capital constraint, so the model is scored on the portfolio rather than on a price forecast.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.19937

### Alberta is bringing in rules to block frivolous data-centre proposals

The Logic

Alberta technology minister Nate Glubish told The Logic on Nov. 14 that the province is preparing rules against speculative data-centre proposals and projects built too close to homes, and that he plans to table legislation as early as this week. He cited Olds, where regulators rejected a Synapse power plant planned about 30 metres from the nearest houses, and the push follows Meta's announced $13-billion campus.

Why it's worth knowing: Alberta has been drawing data-centre filings, and the minister is saying proposals without a real plan, or sited next to houses, will not get through.

https://thelogic.co/news/exclusive/alberta-new-rules-block-frivolous-data-centres/

### Ontario needs a moratorium on AI data centres before it’s too late

Canada's National Observer

Ontario Green Party leader Mike Schreiner and MPP Aislinn Clancy are calling for a moratorium on AI data centres until the province has enforceable rules on community and Indigenous consent, environmental review, and electricity. In a Nov. 16 column, Schreiner wrote that the government's data-centre playbook sets no binding limits or investment and job targets.

Why it's worth knowing: Cities including Mississauga have already paused projects. This is a call for a province-wide stop until the rules contain limits a community can enforce.
