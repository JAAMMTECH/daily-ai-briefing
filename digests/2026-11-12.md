### Gemini Decisions API

Hacker News

Google introduced a Gemini Decisions API, backed by a Gemini Decision 1 model, that scores up to 1,000 closed-set questions in one pass instead of writing an answer token by token. It accepts text, images, audio, video, and PDFs, returns a probability and a confidence score in about 150 to 250 milliseconds end to end, and is priced at $0.10 per million input tokens with no charge for output tokens.

Why it's worth knowing: Routing, policy checks, and rubric scores can stay fast and cheap, and a confidence number gives code a reason to hand an uncertain case to a person.

Terms:
- **API** — application programming interface — A defined way for one program to send a request to another service and get a structured result back.

https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/
Discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50456791

### United Airlines bans AI agents from booking flights on behalf of travelers

Hacker News

United Airlines said it will block third-party AI agents from booking flights or otherwise acting inside a traveler's account. It pointed to the risk of sharing payment and login details, unwanted purchases or mileage redemptions, and federal rules that require travelers to confirm their own baggage.

Why it's worth knowing: A major carrier is treating an agent that holds credentials and takes actions as a different problem from a search site that only displays fares.

Terms:
- **AI agent** — artificial intelligence agent — Software that pursues a goal by taking actions, such as booking a ticket, rather than only drafting an answer.

https://www.cbsnews.com/news/united-airlines-bans-ai-agents-booking-flights/

### My AI agents found an unmapped Maya site?

Hacker News

A developer described AI agents that scanned public lidar of the Maya region and flagged a hilltop site missing from Mexico's INAH register. An update on November 11 said archaeologists had already mapped it in a 2025 paper, which the agents missed while reviewing the literature.

Why it's worth knowing: A gap in an official register is not a discovery if the prior paper is sitting in the literature the agent was supposed to check.

Terms:
- **lidar** — light detection and ranging — A laser scan that maps the ground, including shapes hidden under forest canopy.
- **INAH** — Instituto Nacional de Antropología e Historia — Mexico's National Institute of Anthropology and History, which keeps the official register of archaeological sites.

https://pietromiotti.com/latest/maya-site

### llama.cpp Gets Major CUDA Improvements: FP8 Support and Faster GPU Inference

r/LocalLLM

A post on r/LocalLLM reports that llama.cpp has added OCP FP8 support, an FP8 key-value cache, and CUDA optimizations aimed at faster GPU inference.

Why it's worth knowing: Eight-bit floats can cut memory use for weights and cache on recent Nvidia cards, which is what determines whether a model fits and how fast it generates.

Terms:
- **FP8** — 8-bit floating point — A compact number format that stores model weights or cache values in eight bits instead of 16.
- **OCP** — Open Compute Project — An industry group whose FP8 formats are one of the standards llama.cpp is adding.

https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLM/comments/1xu9vmj/llamacpp_gets_major_cuda_improvements_fp8_support/

### Huawei releases MACaQ, its internal quantizer for local inference

Hacker News

Huawei released MACaQ, an open quantizer that builds low-bit checkpoints for text, image, video, and audio models, including GGUF files that run in llama.cpp. A companion, MACaQ-R, refines an existing GGUF so the improved file still loads in a standard llama.cpp build.

Why it's worth knowing: Local runners can shop for a smaller file, or repair one they already have, without switching away from the runtime they use now.

Terms:
- **GGUF** — GPT-Generated Unified Format — The file format llama.cpp uses to store a quantized model for local inference.

https://github.com/huawei-csl/MACaQ

r/LocalLLM

Unsloth released Dynamic v3.1 GGUFs of Qwen3.8-27B and says they are its highest-accuracy quantizations of that model, ahead by about 5 to 10 percent on average across Terminal Bench, DeepSWE, and other coding benchmarks. The files are aimed at machines with 12 GB of RAM or more.

### Show HN: Yogurt – Free, private, open-source alternative to Granola

Hacker News

Yogurt is a free, MIT-licensed desktop app that records a meeting without a bot in the call, then transcribes and summarizes it on the computer. It uses whisper.cpp, Apple's on-device SpeechAnalyzer where available, and a local model through llama.cpp, and it can share notes with another assistant only through a local MCP server.

Why it's worth knowing: Meeting notes are a common reason people send audio to a cloud product, and this keeps the recording, transcript, and summary on the machine.

Terms:
- **MCP** — Model Context Protocol — A standard way for an assistant app to read tools and data, here the notes stored on the same computer.

https://github.com/FrigadeHQ/yogurt
Discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50454625

### Toronto startup ChainPatrol uses AI to take down crypto impersonation sites

MoneyWise

MoneyWise profiled Toronto startup ChainPatrol, which scans one to two million new websites a day for pages impersonating cryptocurrency brands. The team uses AI and analysts to blocklist fakes in as little as 15 minutes and to seek takedowns in about 24 hours.

Why it's worth knowing: Scam sites can now be cloned in minutes, so the useful measure is how fast a warning or takedown goes up, not how careful a weekly review is.

Terms:
- **blocklist** — block list — A list of addresses that browsers and security tools warn on or refuse to open.
