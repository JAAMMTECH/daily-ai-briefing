# AI + Ontario briefing — November 7, 2026

AI labs are using their own models to speed research, while local servers get faster at swapping models and a new video model gets cheaper. In Canada, the budget’s compute plan, a new online regulator, and more Ontario STEM seats are the changes that decide who can build and who sets the rules.

## AI

### AI is dramatically accelerating AI research

Hacker News

A reading of a METR investigation argues Anthropic has already hit its own test for AI dramatically speeding up AI research. From October 2025 to October 2026, an internal capabilities index rose about twice as fast as in the prior year, and the authors treat automation of research as most of why that pace is still holding.

Why it's worth knowing: If software progress is really doubling, timelines move even when the chip supply does not, because the same researchers finish more of the work.

Terms:
- **METR** — Model Evaluation and Threat Research — An independent group that measures what AI systems can do and how risky those abilities are.

https://www.planned-obsolescence.org/p/ai-is-dramatically-accelerating-ai
Discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50397994

### Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash

Hacker News

Google made Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash generally available in the Gemini API and Gemini for business. It can generate and edit video natively for up to 15 seconds, up from 10, and the 720p price is $0.07 per second, 30 percent below Omni 1.1.

Why it's worth knowing: A follow-up edit can fix one detail instead of regenerating the whole clip, which is a cheaper way to keep a product or character consistent across a short ad.

Terms:
- **API** — application programming interface — The developer doorway for asking the model to generate or edit a clip from another program.

https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/

### A Jev that can ask Opus for help gives Opus accuracy at 4x lower cost

Hacker News

Hightouch describes a smaller judge, called a Jev, that can hand hard cases to Opus. On an identity-resolution task, that setup matched Opus-level accuracy at about four times lower cost.

Why it's worth knowing: Sending only the uncertain calls to the expensive model is a way to keep a strong judgment without paying its rate on every example.

Terms:
- **Opus** — Claude Opus — Anthropic’s larger, more expensive model, used here as the helper the smaller judge can call.

https://hightouch.com/blog/llm-judges-identity-resolution
Discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50397429

### Swapping LLMs in Under Two Seconds: A Sleep Mode for Llama.cpp

Hacker News (watchlist)

A llama.cpp note shows how to park a model’s GPU memory in ordinary RAM instead of shutting the model down when llama-swap switches models. Swaps that took 10.3 seconds fell to about 2.1 seconds, and to 1.7 seconds with multi-size transparent huge pages, while the cached prompt stayed in place.

Why it's worth knowing: Anyone alternating a voice model and a larger model on one GPU pays that wait on every handoff, and also avoids rebuilding a long system prompt.

Terms:
- **llama.cpp** — a local inference engine for language models — Software that runs quantized models on your own machine, including through its llama-server.
- **GPU** — graphics processing unit — The card that holds the model weights during generation. Parking copies that memory to the computer’s RAM so another model can use the card.

https://zaage.it/writing/llama-sleep/
Discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50399642

### ggml-org/llama.cpp b12188

GitHub: ggml-org/llama.cpp

llama.cpp b12188 visits parent slots before child slots that share a prompt prefix. A child request that landed on a lower slot number no longer waits an extra batch for its parent to wake it.

Why it's worth knowing: Shared prefixes are how a server skips reprocessing the same prompt, and this bug made that shortcut one batch slower whenever the child was queued first.

https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b12188

### [Release] Qwen3.8-Flash-Next DR-V2-Q2_0 GGUF - Fits in 48GB VRAM (38.4 GB Backbone), 83.3% AIME 2025, 74.7% Aider Polyglot

r/LocalLLM

A new Qwen3.8-Flash-Next GGUF, DR-V2-Q2_0, keeps a 38.4 GB backbone inside 48 GB of VRAM and leaves a 28.8 GB lookup table in system RAM or on an SSD. The author reports 83.3 percent on AIME 2025 and 74.7 percent on Aider Polyglot, using standard integer quant types so it runs in stock llama.cpp.

Why it's worth knowing: Many ultra-low-bit files need special lookup tables that slow the prompt pass. This one claims about 3.4 times faster prefill than those IQ-grid quants at a similar size.

Terms:
- **GGUF** — GPT-Generated Unified Format — The file format llama.cpp uses for a quantized model, so weights can run on a consumer GPU.
- **VRAM** — video random-access memory — Memory on the graphics card. Weights that fit there generate tokens much faster than weights fetched from system RAM.

https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLM/comments/1xpim04/release_qwen38flashnext_drv2q2_0_gguf_fits_in/

Watchlist match.

### Towards Foundation Decision Models: Drex

Hugging Face papers

Drex is a family of open models that, given a document or agent context and a question with options, return a probability over those options instead of writing out a chain of thought. The 27B model, Max, scores 67.52 on the public suite of Decision Index 0.4.1, against a 60 in the same comparison.

Why it's worth knowing: A system that only needs a label, a yes, or a score pays extra delay and gets brittle text when a generative model has to write the decision.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.07854

## Ontario and Canada

### Ottawa and Silicon Valley are both after Canadian AI processing power. Who will win?

The Globe and Mail

The Globe and Mail reports that Thursday’s budget would use federal backstops and offtake agreements to help build 500 megawatts of AI compute for Canadian companies. Ottawa would commit to buy part of a new data centre’s output or to underwrite its debt, the same style of support the Canada Growth Fund and Canada Infrastructure Bank have used on other large projects.

Why it's worth knowing: A purchase guarantee is a different bet than another research grant: it decides whether Canadian firms can actually rent the machines they need.

Terms:
- **offtake agreement** — a contract to buy a project’s output — Promising to purchase compute lets a data-centre developer borrow against that revenue and start construction.

https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-ottawa-silicon-valley-after-canadian-ai-processing-power-who-will-win/

### Ottawa earmarks $176 million to establish online super regulator

BetaKit

Budget 2026 sets aside $176.3 million over five years for a Digital Safety and Data Protection Commission of Canada. BetaKit reports it would enforce private-sector privacy law and the Safe Social Media Act, and could fine social-media and chatbot companies up to $10 million or 3 percent of global revenue.

Why it's worth knowing: Products that put a chatbot in front of Canadians would answer to a regulator that can compel audits and issue binding orders, not only publish guidance.

https://betakit.com/ottawa-earmarks-176-million-to-establish-online-super-regulator/

### Federal Budget 2026: live coverage of the tech, AI and telecom measures

The Circuit

The Circuit’s budget notes say Ottawa will draft a Bank Act change so customers owe nothing on an unauthorized transaction unless they were grossly negligent. The same chapter says every university, college, and CEGEP student should get access to trusted AI agents, with the details due before the end of 2026.

Why it's worth knowing: One measure moves fraud losses onto the institutions that run the pipes, and the other would put a public AI tool in front of students before schools share a rulebook for it.

Terms:
- **CEGEP** — Collège d'enseignement général et professionnel — Quebec’s public colleges, which sit between high school and university and are included in the student AI-agent plan.

https://thecircuit.ca/posts/2026-11-05-federal-budget-2026-live/

### The feds plan to merge BDC and EDC. What might that mean for Canada’s largest VC investor?

BetaKit

Ottawa plans to merge the Business Development Bank of Canada and Export Development Canada, and it says Crown corporations that finance businesses should take more risk.

Why it's worth knowing: BDC is Canada’s largest venture investor, so the merger changes who writes cheques for startups and how willing that lender is to lose some of them.

Terms:
- **BDC** — Business Development Bank of Canada — A federal Crown corporation that lends to smaller firms and is the country’s biggest venture-capital investor.
- **EDC** — Export Development Canada — The Crown corporation that finances and insures Canadian companies selling abroad. It would be combined with BDC.

https://betakit.com/the-feds-plan-to-merge-bdc-and-edc-what-might-that-mean-for-canadas-largest-vc-investor/

### Ontario Funding More Than 6,400 Additional Seats in London to Prepare Students for In-Demand Careers

Ontario Newsroom

Ontario is funding more than 6,400 additional post-secondary seats in London. They are part of a $1.7 billion investment for more than 70,000 seats in STEM, health care, education, and the trades.

Why it's worth knowing: The province is adding classroom places in the fields technology employers hire from, with London as one of the first visible chunks of that expansion.

Terms:
- **STEM** — science, technology, engineering, and mathematics — The cluster of programs the province named, alongside health care, education, and skilled trades, for the new seats.

https://news.ontario.ca/en/release/1008250/ontario_funding_more_than_6400_additional_seats_in_london_to_prepare_students_for_in_demand_careers
