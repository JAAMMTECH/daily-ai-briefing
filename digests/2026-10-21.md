### eMudhra intermediate CA compromise

Hacker News

Mozilla and researchers reported a compromise of an eMudhra intermediate certificate authority, and the post drew nearly a thousand points and hundreds of comments on Hacker News.

Why it's worth knowing: A compromised CA can let attackers issue trusted certificates for sites they do not own, which is why browsers act quickly on these.

Terms:
- **CA** — Certificate Authority — An organization that signs digital certificates vouching that a public key belongs to a given domain. Browsers ship a list of trusted CAs and accept certificates chained to them.
- **intermediate CA** — Intermediate Certificate Authority — A CA whose certificate is signed by a root CA and which in turn signs end-site certificates. If its key is compromised, anyone holding it can mint certificates that browsers will trust.

https://emudhra.we-have-a-problem.com/
Discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50166579

### FP16 GEMM Optimization on AMD RDNA 3 and RDNA 4 Architectures

Hacker News (watchlist)

AMD walks through building an FP16 matrix-multiply kernel for RDNA 3, 3.5 and 4 GPUs in eight steps, ending within a few percent of hipBLASLt. It measures which optimizations help one architecture and hurt another, including on the Radeon 8060S.

Why it's worth knowing: It is a concrete guide to how local-inference performance is tuned on consumer AMD hardware.

Terms:
- **GEMM** — General Matrix Multiply — The core operation C = A × B that dominates neural network compute. Libraries and GPU kernels are heavily tuned for it.
- **RDNA** — Radeon DNA (AMD's GPU architecture family) — AMD's consumer and workstation GPU architecture line. RDNA 3, 3.5 and 4 are successive generations.
- **hipBLASLt** — Project name for ROCm's tuned GEMM library (HIP BLAS Lightweight) — A ROCm library of optimized matrix-multiply routines, used here as the performance baseline.

https://rocm.blogs.amd.com/software-tools-optimization/rdna-fp16-gemm/README.html
Discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50167109

### Show HN: A layer on top llama.cpp enabling big MoE streaming on phone and laptop

Hacker News (watchlist)

BigMoeOnEdge is a layer over llama.cpp that keeps hot weights in memory and streams Mixture-of-Experts experts from storage. It reports a 35B model at about 10 tok/s on a 12 GB phone, with output identical to fully in-memory runs.

Why it's worth knowing: It points to a way to run models far larger than RAM on ordinary devices, though the figures come from the author.

Terms:
- **MoE** — Mixture of Experts — A model design where each token activates only a few expert sub-networks, so most weights sit idle per step. That makes it possible to stream inactive experts from disk.
- **llama.cpp** — Project name, not an acronym. The .cpp means it is written in C++ — A widely used open-source engine for running quantized LLMs locally on CPUs and GPUs.
- **tok/s** — Tokens per second — The usual measure of LLM generation speed.

https://github.com/Helldez/BigMoeOnEdge
Discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50168132

### Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B, NVIDIA's own IQ2_XXS 24GB GGUF, on an RTX 5090 with llama.cpp b11750 at 32K and 64K context: tok/s with and without the LiLiCorr drafter, VRAM measured, and 33 DevOps tool-use tasks next to 8 open models (Q4) measured the same day

r/LocalLLM

A user benchmarked NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B at 2-bit quantization on an RTX 5090, reporting tokens per second and VRAM at 32K and 64K context, with and without a drafter, plus 33 DevOps tool-use tasks against eight other models.

Why it's worth knowing: It is a real measured result for running a large MoE model on a single consumer GPU.

Terms:
- **IQ2_XXS** — Importance-matrix 2-bit quantization, extra-extra-small — An aggressive llama.cpp quantization format that stores weights in about two bits to shrink model size, at some cost in quality.
- **drafter** — Draft model for speculative decoding — A small model proposes several tokens that the large model verifies in one pass, increasing speed without changing the output distribution.
- **VRAM** — Video Random Access Memory — Memory on the GPU that holds model weights and the context cache.

https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLM/comments/1xap599/nemotron_35_bolt_74ba9b_nvidias_own_iq2_xxs_24gb/
