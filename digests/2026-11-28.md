### Google's DeepCrossAttention achieves comparable model quality up to 3x faster

Hacker News

On C4 and the One Billion Word benchmark, Google's November 23 post says DeepCrossAttention (DCA) matched a standard transformer in up to five times fewer training steps. The post also says the same quality can arrive up to three times faster with a negligible parameter increase, and, in another comparison, with up to 30 to 40 percent fewer parameters.

Why it's worth knowing: This is a new write-up of an ICML 2025 paper, and the excerpt cuts off before the image-classification results.

Terms:
- **DCA** — DeepCrossAttention — A transformer change that lets each layer weight outputs from earlier layers instead of adding a residual with equal weight.
- **C4** — Colossal Cleaned Common Crawl — The cleaned web-text corpus used for the language-model training comparison in the post.

https://research.google/blog/supercharging-transformer-residual-connections/

### Solve Vision with Code: Coding Agents Are Stronger Visual Reasoners Than Video Models

Hugging Face papers

Given one attempt and no ground truth, three coding agents that render an answer video by writing a program scored 0.923 with Codex and gpt-6-astra, 0.894 with Claude Code and Fable 5.1, and 0.893 with Gemini CLI and Gemini 3.1 Pro on the 500-task VBVR-Pro-Bench. The excerpt ends before the video models' scores on those same tasks.

Why it's worth knowing: The test holds the task fixed and changes only whether the answer is drawn by a video model or by code.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34813

### DRBench Pro: A Knowledge-Centric Benchmark for Professional Deep Research Agents

Hugging Face papers

DRBench Pro judges a research report by coverage of a Wikipedia-derived knowledge tree, with 144 cases, 24 domains, and 44,102 rubrics. The best of 16 agents reached 57.04 percent overall, the geometric mean of breadth across directions and depth along related chains.

Why it's worth knowing: A fluent report can still miss whole branches of a question, which is the gap this score is built to show.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34852

### Revisiting LLMs as Context Engineers

Hugging Face papers

On 8-billion-parameter models, no context-engineering method won across the benchmark, including against chain-of-thought, and extra parameters or generation steps did not make the gains consistent. Gains were negatively correlated (r=-0.5) with how diverse the failed reasoning traces were.

Why it's worth knowing: The measured pattern is that a method's gain shrinks as the failures become less alike.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.30494

## Ontario and Canada

### Budget office tells committee it knows of no stated return on borrowed $1.6-billion Cohere stake

The Hub

Annette Ryan of the Office of the Parliamentary Budget Officer told MPs the $1.6-billion Cohere equity stake is borrowed in full, is recorded at its purchase price until the shares are sold, and does not count against either fiscal anchor. She said she knows of no statement of the expected return, and that her office has not received an investment analysis.

Why it's worth knowing: Debt-service costs still affect the deficit, so a stake that misses both anchors is not outside the budget.

https://thehub.ca/2026/11/28/budget-office-tells-committee-it-knows-of-no-stated-return-on-borrowed-1-6-billion-cohere-stake/

### Q&A: AI Minister Evan Solomon on why Canadians won’t know the terms of the feds’ $1.6-billion Cohere investment

BetaKit

AI Minister Evan Solomon told BetaKit the Cohere stake is meant to keep the company and its intellectual property in Canada, to let Canadians share the upside, and to give the government a seat at the table. He said the commercial terms stay unpublished because they are sensitive and because releasing them would weaken this negotiation and others, which are done through the Canada Development Investment Corporation.

Why it's worth knowing: That is the government's reason for secrecy, set beside the budget office's statement that it has seen no expected return.

https://betakit.com/qa-ai-minister-evan-solomon-on-why-canadians-wont-know-the-terms-of-the-feds-1-6-billion-cohere-investment/

### Air Creebec warns of cyber attack that compromised personal information of passengers, customers, employees

CBC News

Air Creebec said an intrusion from June 23 to July 6 copied personal information to the dark web; the Ontario-and-Quebec airline learned of it on October 22 and published the notice on November 26. People who paid by card from 2000 through 2023 are affected, and employee records may include a social insurance number, banking, and health data, but not passport numbers or card PINs.

Why it's worth knowing: The company is offering credit monitoring, and the notice does not describe the intrusion as an AI attack.

https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/sudbury/air-creebec-airline-cyber-attack-hacking-dark-web-personal-information-breach-security-9.7438193
