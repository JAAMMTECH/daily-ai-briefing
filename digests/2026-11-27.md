### A bare-metal OS running Qwen3-0.6B 54% faster than llama.cpp

Hacker News (watchlist)

A 24,252-byte bare-metal operating system boots in 74 to 94 milliseconds and runs Qwen3-0.6B in Q8_0 from RAM, beating llama.cpp on one AMD EPYC 7642 by 42 percent at one thread (23.67 versus 16.63 tokens per second), 54 percent at four (78.89 versus 51.08), 46 percent at eight (118.08 versus 80.79) and 31 percent at sixteen (134.94 versus 103.03). The output matched all 64 greedy tokens against PyTorch FP32, with a cosine of 0.99965, and stayed bit-exact across thread counts.

Why it's worth knowing: The measured gain is a CPU result for a 0.6 billion parameter model in the same 8-bit format on both sides.

Terms:
- **Q8_0** — 8-bit GGUF quantization — The weight format used on both sides of this comparison, so the speed gap is not from a smaller file.

### RustingStrata – Pure Rust LLM GGUF Engine, Runs Models > VRAM on CUDA and Vulkan

Hacker News (watchlist)

RustingStrata 1.4.1 is a Rust engine for GGUF models that compiles its CUDA kernels at runtime and can run those same kernels on Vulkan, executing experts that do not fit in video memory on the CPU at the same time. On an RTX 3060 with 12 GB it lines up a Qwen3.6-35B-A3B file that fits in neither its memory nor llama.cpp's, but the tokens-per-second figures are cut off in the excerpt, and the notes say a vLLM bf16 column is not comparable to its Q4_K_M run.

Why it's worth knowing: Mixture-of-experts files are now larger than a typical gaming GPU, so the practical question is how the overflow is scheduled, and this write-up does not yet show a speed.

### ggml-org/llama.cpp b12811

GitHub: ggml-org/llama.cpp

llama.cpp build b12811 adds a Share action in Llama Chat that uploads a conversation, or the branch up to one message, to a Hugging Face dataset as a JSONL agent trace for the Hub's trace viewer. Sign-in is Hugging Face OAuth with PKCE, and the app may create datasets and write only to the ones it created, or the user can paste a token.

### Google Will Make AI Agents Use Its MCP Servers. The Gmail One Can't Send Email

Hacker News

From 1 February 2027, a tool that is an AI agent, or is driven by one, must reach Gmail, Drive, Calendar and the rest of Workspace through Google's MCP servers, and agents that keep calling the REST APIs are held to standard quotas. The public Gmail server can search, read, create a draft and apply labels, but sending, replying, forwarding and editing a draft stay partner-only; Google can also cap embeddings and other memory built from Workspace data at 30 days, and it bans training on that data.

Why it's worth knowing: Anthropic's Workspace connector already advertises send, reply and forward, which an independent tool using the same user grant cannot do on the public server.

Terms:
- **MCP** — Model Context Protocol — The tool interface Google will require agents to use for Workspace.
- **REST** — representational state transfer — The ordinary HTTP APIs that agents may still call, but only inside standard quotas and against the new terms.

https://techstackups.com/articles/google-workspace-ai-agents-mcp/

### ShowNow: Fine-Grained Speculative Streaming for Responsive World Models

Hugging Face papers

ShowNow's 5-billion-parameter world model sustains 480p video at 16 frames per second on one H100 while keeping eight action-conditioned futures prepared. In ViZDoom the full deployment cuts median input-to-display latency from 620 to 208 milliseconds versus native-block SolarWM-FT, and a matched-policy test cuts restart empty ticks from 1.56 percent to 0.09 percent.

Why it's worth knowing: For a model you steer while it plays, the wait between an action and the next frame matters as much as the frame rate.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34693

### Stable Gated DeltaNet-2: Understanding and Mitigating Recurrent State Instability

Hugging Face papers

The paper traces Gated DeltaNet-2's long-context training crashes to erase steps that amplify the running state even when nothing new is written, and it switches to symmetric erasure plus a decay-regulated residual write. At about 1.6 billion parameters, that raises the 128K average RULER score by 6.80 points in the recurrent setting and average accuracy on nine downstream tasks by 0.62 points in the hybrid setting.

Why it's worth knowing: A linear-attention model stays cheap only if its fixed state does not explode, and this is a measured repair for that failure.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34051

### OS-Omni: Benchmarking Generalist Computer-Using Agents on Long-Horizon Workflows Across Platforms

Hugging Face papers

OS-Omni v1 is a live agent test with 48 tasks on each of Windows, Ubuntu, macOS, iOS, Android and the web, plus 12 workflows that pair a desktop with an Android phone, and people needed 67.54 steps on average against an OSWorld reference of 15. The strongest model's score is cut off in the excerpt.

Why it's worth knowing: Tasks this long will not reward an agent that only looks competent on a short click script, but the excerpt does not show who won.

### What a Forecasting Leaderboard Can Establish: Resolution Reporting for Zero-Shot Time-Series Models

Hugging Face papers

Vernier, a check on forecasting leaderboards, finds that GIFT-Eval can resolve only 6.8 rank places while neighbouring entries sit 0.91 apart, so its top ten hold fewer than two real steps, and that fev-bench, Chronos Benchmark II and TIME are 3.6 to 19 times too coarse to separate neighbours. Across four boards, rules that rank against the pool reversed 43 top-ten pairs in one update in seven without rerunning either model; BOOM, treating each of 2,554 series as its own source, is the board that resolves its top ten.

Why it's worth knowing: A one-place gap on a public forecasting board can be smaller than the board is able to measure.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.31619

## Ontario and Canada

### Government of Canada boosts the AI Compute Access Fund with a $700 million expansion

supplemental

On November 26 in Toronto, AI Minister Evan Solomon announced the next phase of the AI Compute Access Fund: $700 million under the National AI Strategy, bringing it from an initial $300 million to $1 billion, with health and life sciences applications open. More than $52 million goes to 45 companies, taking commitments past $118 million for 89 companies, toward compute that is owned, operated and governed in Canada and a 500-megawatt sovereign-compute pledge.

Why it's worth knowing: This is the pool Canadian firms can apply to now, and the first open call is health and life sciences rather than a general round.

https://www.canada.ca/en/innovation-science-economic-development/news/2026/11/government-of-canada-boosts-the-ai-compute-access-fund-with-700-million-expansion.html

### Ottawa begins rolling out $700-million in AI compute funding under a new sector-based approach

supplemental

The Globe and Mail's account of that announcement says the fund will cover four-fifths of compute costs when the provider is Canadian-incorporated, controlled and owned, and that tools from Toronto-based Cohere will help analyze and risk-assess applications while officials make the final call. Eligibility would widen to non-profits and postsecondary institutes, and Ottawa plans to launch every sector call, starting with health and life sciences, by the end of 2027.

Why it's worth knowing: The reimbursement rate decides whether a buyer has a reason to choose a Canadian-owned operator, and a Toronto company's model would sit in the review path.

Terms:
- **four-fifths** — 80 percent — The share of eligible compute costs the Globe says the fund will cover for a Canadian-owned provider.

### What would happen if an AI agent interfered with a Canadian election?

supplemental

Chief Electoral Officer Stéphane Perrault told a Commons committee that the Elections Act has no provision for an AI agent that meddles without a person directing it, because civil and criminal liability still attaches to people and corporations. Elections Canada's site is under constant attack but has not been breached, he said, and the office is starting to format its pages so AI systems pick them up; Commissioner Caroline Simard called the no-human case a hypothetical her office would need to study.

Why it's worth knowing: An agent that posts or spends on its own still needs a person or a company behind it before the current act has someone to charge.

Terms:
- **Elections Act** — Canada Elections Act — The federal election statute Perrault says still assumes a human or corporate actor.

https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/canadian-elections-artificial-intelligence-9.7436972

### Direction on Government of Canada Cyber Security Readiness in the Frontier Artificial Intelligence Era

supplemental

A federal security notice in effect since October 1 tells departments to assume they will be breached and to patch faster, because frontier models can find vulnerabilities, generate zero-day exploits and chain attacks at a new speed and scale. Departments are expected to file a prioritized list of critical services, and the systems behind them, in the Treasury Board Secretariat's portfolio tool by November 30.

Why it's worth knowing: The date that matters this week is November 30, when those critical-service lists are due.
