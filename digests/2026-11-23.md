### Clover-35B-A3B-Preview Technical Report

Hugging Face papers

Clover-35B-A3B-Preview is a mixture-of-experts diffusion language model with 35 billion parameters and 3 billion active. The report says it leads the seven agent metrics it lists, including 73.6 on SWE-bench Verified and 51.7 on Terminal-Bench 2.1, and cites 680 tokens per second on one NVIDIA GB300 at BF16 and batch size one.

Why it's worth knowing: The speed figure is for a single GPU at full BF16 precision, alongside scores on the coding-agent tests the report uses.

Terms:
- **BF16** — brain float 16 — A 16-bit number format for training and inference. The quoted speed is at this precision, on one GPU, with a batch of one.

### In Search of Perfection: aci-cpp vs. llama.cpp v1.0.0

Hacker News (watchlist)

Actual's aci-cpp fork of llama.cpp beat llama.cpp v1.0.0 on eight of ten workloads by up to 28 percent, on Qwen3.8-27B at Q4_K_M. On a DGX Spark, a follow-up with 250K tokens of context returned the first token in 1.79 seconds versus 2.10, and at 64K in 0.78 seconds versus 0.97.

Why it's worth knowing: The same build served 19 to 37 percent more throughput on 4,096-token prompts, and on an M3 Ultra it decoded a 250K context at 12.6 tokens per second, 46 percent above Actual's previous client.

https://actual.inc/blog/in-search-of-perfection

### Show HN: Gall – Inkling 2 Mini on 24GB M2, frees RAM between messages

Hacker News (watchlist)

Gall runs Inkling-2-Mini, a 26-billion-parameter mixture of experts with 3.4 billion active, on a 24 GB M2 MacBook Air from an Unsloth UD-IQ3_XXS GGUF, at about 20 tokens per second. Thinking Machines lists 32 GB as the Mac minimum; Gall unloads the model about a second after each answer and the author reports about 50 MB of RAM between messages.

Why it's worth knowing: The new piece is the engine: Rust with Metal kernels, an OpenAI-compatible endpoint on the laptop, and a saved cache so the next message can skip a full reload of the prompt.

Terms:
- **GGUF** — GPT-generated unified format — The file format Gall reads in place for the quantized Inkling weights. This build uses Unsloth's UD-IQ3_XXS file, about 12.7 GB to download.

https://github.com/ltrgoddard/gall

### Non-penetrating World Models

Hugging Face papers

NoPeWorld trains a small head on a frozen diffusion transformer's hidden states to flag actions that would push an object through another, then replaces those actions with safer ones. Averaged over six open video world models, the physical-violation rate fell from 74 percent to 16 percent, including on open-domain images, third-person views, and moving obstacles.

Why it's worth knowing: The paper's claim is that these video models already encode the collision, so a lightweight readout can catch it without retraining the generator.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.27963

### Lost in the Hallway: Benchmarking Persistent Spatial Memory Across Revisits

Hugging Face papers

ReSpaceBench asks whether a model still recognizes a room after leaving and coming back, with 3,244 questions across 590 home scenes and nine tasks. The strongest zero-shot model scored 50.5 percent overall, against 90.1 percent for human annotators, and the agent setups tested did not beat direct inference with the same base model.

Why it's worth knowing: Remembering a physical place across visits is a basic job for an assistant that returns to the same home, and the gap to people is still about 40 points.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.21485
