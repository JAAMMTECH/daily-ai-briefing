### GPT-6 models demonstrate intrinsic protein-design capabilities

Hacker News

OpenAI says the GPT-6 family can design new protein shapes and amino-acid sequences using reasoning and ordinary code, without calling a specialized biology model to generate the designs. In tests with Onava, proteins from GPT-6.2's designs showed binding on five targets and redesigned antibodies still bound, while predicting a full protein structure remained a clear weakness.

Why it's worth knowing: Prompts set constraints as small as one bond or as large as the protein's overall shape, without retraining. Specialized models still supplied sequences and chose which designs to test, so the lab step was not GPT-6 alone.

### Serving Qwen via AWS Lambdas for fun and 80x negative profit

Hacker News

An experiment split H2O-Lightning, a fine-tune of Qwen3.5-4B, into 32 layers of about 225 MB so each layer fit in a 3 GB AWS Lambda, about 5,600 jobs for one document. A 4,000-token read took 17 seconds and 3.4 cents, or $8.40 per million tokens, against $0.10 per million for Gemini Decision 1.

Why it's worth knowing: The fan-out does run a model that will not fit in one function, and the bill is still more than 80 times the hosted price, because that work is a matrix multiply.

Terms:
- **Lambda** — AWS Lambda — A short-lived cloud function. This run capped each one at 3 GB, so one layer fit and the full 9.3 GB model did not.

https://noahbuilt.it/writing/qwen-on-5600-lambdas

### PenGPT: A Generative Transformer for Handwriting and Sketches

Hacker News

PenGPT, from a November 12 post that reached Hacker News today, is a 6-million-parameter model that writes print and cursive and picks up an unseen hand from a few words, plus a 54-million-parameter model that draws from a text prompt. The handwriting set is 495,590 words by 1,255 writers, and training on one word at a time moves style rank from 1.9 to 6.0.

Why it's worth knowing: Data and how it is shown did the work. A vocabulary sixty times larger and a doubled model changed nothing, byte-pair merges only shortened sequences by 3.2 times while making the score slightly worse, and one head in layer 4 put 81% of its attention on the letter being written.

https://greydanus.github.io/2026/11/12/pengpt/

### Rewirable Circuits in Transformers with Loom Attention

Hugging Face papers

Loom attention rewires a transformer at every token by mixing a head's attention over earlier heads with its attention over tokens. On the same 300 billion tokens and about 2% extra FLOPs, LoomPythia-2.8B made about 1.6 times the downstream gain of mHC-Pythia-2.8B over Pythia-2.8B, and matched Pythia-12B at zero shot.

Why it's worth knowing: The paper also says LoomFormer matches plain transformers trained with more than 3 times the compute, and that at five examples it surpasses Pythia-12B.

Terms:
- **FLOP** — floating-point operation — One unit of arithmetic. The Loom run used about 2% more of them than the Pythia comparison on the same 300 billion tokens.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.30634

### S-Mind: Internalizing Multi-View Geometry for Spatial Intelligence

Hugging Face papers

S-Mind trains a multimodal model to hold one fused 3D scene in a small set of spatial tokens and re-emit that scene from a new viewpoint. It lifted Qwen3.5 backbones by up to 22.26 points, with probe scores of 65.11% for fusion and 82.17% for a viewpoint change, against at most 16.02% and 37.28% for the baselines.

Why it's worth knowing: On the reported average it sits 0.45 above GPT-6-Astra, 1.97 above Kimi-K3.1-Pro, and 7.80 above GLM-5.3-Flash. The probes are the sharper claim: the baselines were barely fusing views at all.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.28649

### ACAP: Asynchronous Coding Agents for Efficient Robot Control

Hugging Face papers

ACAP treats one robot as several persistent coding agents, each running a control group such as an arm and its gripper, with a hyper-agent assigning tasks and dependencies. Across 72 real-world rollouts in 25 settings, task-balanced time fell 28.2% versus one agent running the whole robot.

Why it's worth knowing: Separate limbs can keep working instead of waiting on a single policy. On all eight collaborative settings it also beat a fixed split of control on mean time, and the excerpt cuts off before that margin.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.31205

## Ontario and Canada

### Transforming surgery through AI

UHN Foundation

University Health Network's Surgical AI Research Academy is using AI to turn routine CT and MRI scans into a patient-specific 3D model of a tumour and the anatomy around it, with breast-cancer surgery as the focus. The November 24 profile reports no accuracy, trial count, or clinical result.

Why it's worth knowing: Surgeons still plan from flat slices and a mental reconstruction. A model from scans a hospital already takes would matter in the consult only if those missing measurements hold up.

Terms:
- **CT** — computed tomography — A cross-sectional scan. The lab also uses MRI, and the story says both are scans hospitals already collect, so the method does not need a new scanner.
- **MRI** — magnetic resonance imaging — The other routine scan the Toronto lab turns into a 3D model of the tumour and nearby anatomy.

https://uhnfoundation.ca/stories/transforming-surgery-through-ai/

### Information Technology and Operational Technology Cybersecurity Source List

CanadaBuys

Defence Construction Canada opened source list MPDAES39 for IT and operational-technology cybersecurity assessments at Department of National Defence sites across Canada. It plans to place three firms on the list for as-and-when-required work, and abbreviated proposals close on January 12, 2027.

Why it's worth knowing: A standing list is how later assessment work can be called without a new open competition each time. At least one of the three firms has to be able to provide the services named in the notice.

Terms:
- **OT** — operational technology — The control systems at a site, as distinct from ordinary office IT. This source list covers cybersecurity assessments of both.

https://www.merx.com/public/solicitations/4207909153/abstract?language=EN
