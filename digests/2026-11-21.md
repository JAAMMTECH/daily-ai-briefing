## AI

### Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights

Hacker News

An open 27B model, given its weights, its graded answers, and the ability to run itself, rewrites those weights with no gradients and no training run, and the edit is 6.6 points more accurate on problems it never saw. Accuracy rose across eight straight sessions, and the model brought a damaged copy back to over 80% of the original accuracy on image tasks it had not seen, without reading the original weights.

Why it's worth knowing: The gain sits in the weights, so it is not rebought as extra compute on every answer. In this model family only the largest model improved itself; the smaller ones made no edit or broke.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21885
Discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50577265

### Vibecoding an inference chip

Hacker News

A developer who says he had never written Verilog used coding agents to run a DeepGrove ternary-weight model on an AWS FPGA with 16 GB of high-bandwidth memory. Measured speed went from 6 to 213 tokens per second, 33 times the first bit-exact full-model baseline, after hundreds of experiments and about $10,000 in Claude and OpenAI tokens.

Why it's worth knowing: The loop was closed on hardware, from a correct but slow design to a rate the author calls real-time, in a language he does not write. Both Astra and Opus were good enough for him to swap between them.

Terms:
- **FPGA** — Field-programmable gate array — A chip whose logic and wiring are loaded as a configuration, rather than fixed when the chip is made.

https://ankitg.me/blog/vibecoding-inference-chip/

### Depth Pro 2: Sharp and Temporally Consistent Metric Monocular Depth

Hugging Face papers

Apple's Depth Pro 2 estimates metric depth for stills and video at the camera's own resolution, from one network instead of separate sharp-but-flickery and steady-but-blurry methods. The authors say the released model family beats earlier work on accuracy, sharpness, temporal consistency, and runtime, and this excerpt does not include the scores.

Why it's worth knowing: Code and weights are public, so the claim can be checked. The design point is that one model takes either a single image or video and does not have to pick between a crisp edge and a stable sequence.

https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26603

### ClawDOS – An AI coding agent for DOS

Hacker News

ClawDOS is a coding agent whose 176 KB program runs on an emulated IBM PC XT, an 8088 at 4.77 MHz with 640K of RAM, and calls Claude itself while a second computer handles the encrypted connection. A primes program it wrote and compiled was on screen 64 seconds after the request, 26 of those seconds spent compiling and running.

Why it's worth knowing: Tool use, notes on disk, and a compiler loop fit in a 1983 PC once encryption is moved off the machine. The timing above is from an emulator of that PC, talking to the live API.

## Ontario and Canada

### Cyber threat actors increasingly use automated and AI-enabled tools in cyber attacks

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security said today that attackers are using automated and AI-enabled tools, including ARTEX, CyberStrikeAI, and PentAGI, to find exposed systems. Of the offensive-tool infrastructure it observed, about 46% was tied to ARTEX, 28% to CyberStrikeAI, and 14% to PentAGI, and ARTEX servers have been aimed at Canadian organizations for several months with no actor named.

Why it's worth knowing: Being scanned from these servers is not a confirmed breach. The centre's point is that people still choose the target, and more of the work between that choice and an intrusion is now done by the tool.

https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/news-events/cyber-threat-actors-increasingly-use-automated-ai-enabled-tools-cyber-attacks

### Canada appoints US-based Financial Data Exchange to set technical standards for open banking

BetaKit

Ottawa is appointing the US-based Financial Data Exchange to set the technical standard for Canada's open banking. The government says it picked FDX because it is the globally recognized standard and the one with the widest support.

Why it's worth knowing: Open banking depends on banks and apps sharing one format. That format will be an existing US-led standard rather than one written only for Canada.

Terms:
- **FDX** — Financial Data Exchange — The organization that publishes the data-sharing standard Canada will use for open banking.

https://betakit.com/canada-to-appoint-us-based-financial-data-exchange-to-set-technical-standards-for-open-banking/

### Regional Artificial Intelligence Initiative — business intake open until November 23

PacifiCan

PacifiCan is taking business applications for the Regional Artificial Intelligence Initiative until noon Pacific time on November 23. The program adds $500 million over five years, $700 million in all, as repayable contributions of $200,000 to $5 million covering up to half of eligible costs.

Why it's worth knowing: British Columbia firms have until Monday. Adoption projects need three years in business and 5 to 500 employees; commercialization projects need one year and 1 to 500.
